# Part 4 Flashcards

Five terms arrived in this Part: subgraph, task-scoped context, contradiction-aware bundle, grounded checker, and decomposed claim. Hide the right-hand column, say each definition out loud, then uncover it and compare.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Subgraph** | A bounded portion of a bigger graph, cut to fit one worker's single task instead of being passed along whole. |
| **Task-scoped context** | The subgraph plus enough framing to say what task it's for — built for relevance to the job at hand, not just for proximity to one node. |
| **Contradiction-aware bundle** | A slice built so that two claim nodes in open conflict both survive the crossing into a worker's context, rather than having the builder quietly declare a winner en route. |
| **Grounded checker** | A checker that verifies a claim against specific edges in a graph, rather than judging the claim by how plausible or well-written its supporting text sounds. |
| **Decomposed claim** | A prose claim broken down into the one specific edge (or edges) whose presence or absence in the graph would actually settle whether the claim is true. |
